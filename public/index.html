FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum wholesale order?

Each article has its own minimum, shown on the product (from 6 pieces for comforters up to 200 for hospital sheets). You can mix sizes. If you order fewer pieces, retail prices apply.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your order list and send the request. You can also message us on WhatsApp. We confirm the price, stock and delivery date with you before taking any payment.

Do you deliver outside Lahore?

Yes, we deliver all over Pakistan by cargo or courier. The delivery charge depends on weight and city, and we tell you before dispatch.

Can you make custom sizes or put our brand label on?

Yes. For private label orders we stitch your label and packaging, make custom sizes and print designs on bulk orders. See Private label.

Will the colours fade?

Our cotton prints use reactive dyes and our stripes are yarn-dyed, so they hold their colour wash after wash. Wash in cold or warm water and dry in the shade.
