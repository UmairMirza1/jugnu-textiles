Questions.

What is the minimum wholesale order?

Each article has its own minimum, shown on the product. It ranges from 6 pieces for comforters to 200 for hospital sheets, and you can mix sizes. Smaller orders are charged at retail prices.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your order list and send the request, or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm the price, stock and delivery date with you before taking any payment.

Do you deliver outside Lahore?

Yes, we deliver all over Pakistan by cargo or courier. We tell you the delivery charge before dispatch.

Can you make custom sizes or use our brand label?

Yes. We offer custom sizes, your own labels and packaging, and custom prints on bulk orders. See Private label.

Will the colours fade?

No. Our cotton prints use reactive dyes and our stripes are yarn-dyed, so they hold their colour wash after wash.
